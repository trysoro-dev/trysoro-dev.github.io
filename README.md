# Soro Notes

Trysoro sells hands-off SEO: it picks keywords, writes the article, and publishes to your site daily. The pitch is clear, so the questions should be too.

**Read the full page:** https://trysoro-dev.github.io/

Soro is aimed squarely at founders who know they need content and have no intention of writing any, and the product is honest about that: add your site, let it learn, let it publish daily. If you have a house style, a compliance reviewer, or a niche where a wrong sentence costs money, an autopilot that ships every day is a liability rather than a feature. The graphs on the homepage are the part to treat carefully, because a rising line labelled started using Soro is a correlation someone chose to screenshot. Supatraffic covers the same job, article plus cover image plus scheduled publishing, and is the alternative this page points at.
